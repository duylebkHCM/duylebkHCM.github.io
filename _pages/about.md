I am an AI researcher and engineer from Vietnam, currently working at the intersection of **generative modeling, multimodal understanding, and controllable visual generation**.

My research began with recognizing content and two-dimensional structure in documents: OCR, table structure recognition, and mathematical expression recognition. These problems taught me how difficult it is for AI systems to understand fine-grained visual patterns, spatial structure, and content constraints. My current work on **document vision-language models** extends that foundation in multimodal understanding. In my latest research, [CONSTANT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07543) (WACV 2026, Oral, Award Finalist), I learned discrete writer-style concepts from a single reference image to guide text-conditioned diffusion, which motivated my interest in composing visual concepts from only a few examples.

Beyond academic research, I have spent several years building AI systems in industry. At Viettel AI, I have worked on OCR, eKYC, document processing, information extraction, synthetic data generation, and efficient VLM inference for real-world applications. This experience has shaped my research style: I care not only about proposing new models, but also about building systems that are robust, scalable, and useful in practice.

Going forward, I want to build generative models that use vision-language understanding to interpret a creator's intent from language, sketches, reference images, and iterative feedback. My PhD goal is **personalized image and video generation** with control over composition, appearance, and motion, while preserving identity and temporal consistency.
